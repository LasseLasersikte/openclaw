# 05 — The Interview Prep Prompt Pack

Walk in already knowing 80% of the questions and having practiced your answers.
The AI becomes your personal interviewer, coach, and researcher. Do this the
night before any interview.

---

## Prompt 1 — Predict your exact questions

```
You are the hiring manager for the job posting below. List the 12 questions you
are most likely to ask a candidate for this specific role: a mix of behavioral,
technical/role-specific, and culture-fit questions. For each, add one sentence
on what you're really trying to learn.

JOB POSTING: [paste]
```

---

## Prompt 2 — Build your STAR stories

Behavioral questions ("Tell me about a time...") are best answered with **STAR**:
Situation, Task, Action, Result.

```
Help me build STAR answers. For each of my accomplishments below, structure a
crisp 60–90 second answer with Situation, Task, Action, Result. Emphasize the
Result with numbers. Keep it conversational, not scripted.

MY ACCOMPLISHMENTS: [paste 3–5 things you're proud of]
```

Build a "story bank" of 5–6 STAR answers. Almost any behavioral question maps to
one of them.

---

## Prompt 3 — Live mock interview

```
Act as the interviewer for [job title] at a [type of company]. Ask me ONE
question at a time and wait for my answer. After each answer, give me brief,
specific feedback (what was strong, what to tighten) before the next question.
Start with a warm-up, then go deeper. Begin now.
```

Type your real answers. This single exercise removes most interview anxiety.

---

## Prompt 4 — The "weakness" and other traps

```
Give me strong, honest answers to these classic traps for the role below, in my
voice, avoiding clichés:
- "What's your greatest weakness?"
- "Why are you leaving your current job?"
- "Tell me about yourself." (a 90-second pitch)
- "Why do you want to work here?"
- "Where do you see yourself in 5 years?"

ROLE: [role]    COMPANY CONTEXT: [one line]    MY BACKGROUND: [one line]
```

---

## Prompt 5 — Research the company fast

```
I have an interview at [company]. Based on what's publicly known, give me:
- A 4-sentence summary of what they do and their likely priorities right now.
- 3 thoughtful, specific questions I can ask the interviewer that show I did my
  homework (not "what's the culture like").
- One way I can connect my background to a challenge they probably face.
```

*(Verify anything factual on the company's actual site before the interview —
AI can be out of date.)*

---

## Prompt 6 — The follow-up thank-you note

```
Write a short, warm thank-you email to send within 24 hours of my interview with
[name, title]. Reference [one specific thing we discussed], reaffirm my interest,
and add one sentence of value. Under 120 words.
```

---

## The night-before checklist

- [ ] Ran the mock interview (Prompt 3) at least once.
- [ ] Have 5 STAR stories ready.
- [ ] Have 3 smart questions to ask them (Prompt 5).
- [ ] Can deliver my "tell me about yourself" in 90 seconds without notes.
- [ ] Thank-you email drafted and ready to personalize and send after.
- [ ] Outfit, tech check (for video), and route/link confirmed.
