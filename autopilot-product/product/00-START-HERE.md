# The AI Job Search Toolkit — Start Here

Welcome. You just bought back **weeks** of your job search. This toolkit turns
the slowest, most demoralizing parts of finding a job — tailoring resumes,
writing cover letters, fixing your LinkedIn, prepping for interviews, and
negotiating pay — into a 20-minute, copy-paste process powered by free AI.

You do **not** need a paid AI subscription. Every prompt here works in the free
tier of ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, or Copilot.

---

## How to use this in the next 30 minutes

1. **Pick your AI.** Open any free AI chat (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Copilot).
2. **Have two things ready:**
   - Your current resume (or just a rough list of your jobs and dates).
   - The exact job posting you're applying to (copy the whole thing).
3. **Work the files in order:**

   | File | What it does | Time |
   |---|---|---|
   | `01-ats-resume-system.md` | Rebuild your resume to pass the screening robots | 15 min |
   | `02-resume-templates.md` | Clean, ATS-safe layouts to paste your content into | 5 min |
   | `03-cover-letter-prompt-pack.md` | A tailored cover letter per job, in 2 minutes | 2 min |
   | `04-linkedin-optimizer.md` | A profile recruiters actually find and click | 20 min |
   | `05-interview-prep-prompt-pack.md` | Rehearse the exact questions you'll get | 30 min |
   | `06-salary-negotiation-scripts.md` | Word-for-word scripts to get paid more | 10 min |
   | `07-application-tracker.csv` | Never lose track of an application again | ongoing |

---

## The one rule that makes all of this work

**Always give the AI the real job posting.** Every prompt in this kit gets
dramatically better when you paste the actual job description, because the AI
mirrors the employer's exact language — which is precisely what the screening
software (and the human) is looking for.

Generic resume in → generic resume out → ignored.
Targeted resume in → interview.

Let's begin. Open `01-ats-resume-system.md`.
