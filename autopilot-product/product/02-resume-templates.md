# 02 — ATS-Safe Resume Templates

Three plain-text layouts the screening robots can read perfectly. Copy the one
that fits your situation into your document editor, drop in the content you
generated in file 01, and you're done. **Keep it one column. Keep it simple.**

---


## Final pre-submit checklist

- [ ] Filename is `First-Last-Resume.pdf` (or .docx if requested) — not `resume_final_v3.pdf`.
- [ ] One page if under 10 years' experience; two pages max otherwise.
- [ ] Every bullet starts with an action verb.
- [ ] At least 5 quantified results.
- [ ] Ran it through the **ATS Score Check (Prompt 4 in file 01)** and scored 85+.
- [ ] No typos — paste it back into AI and ask: *"Proofread for spelling,
      grammar, and consistency. List only the errors."*
