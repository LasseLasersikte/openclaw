# 04 — The LinkedIn Optimizer

Recruiters search LinkedIn like a database. If your profile uses the right words
in the right places, you show up — and opportunities come to *you*. This is the
closest thing to passive job hunting that exists. 20 minutes, one time, pays off
for months.

---

## The headline (the single highest-leverage field)

Your headline follows you everywhere on LinkedIn and is heavily weighted in
search. Don't waste it on just your job title.

```
Write me 5 LinkedIn headline options (max 220 characters each). Formula:
[Role/Title] | [What I help with or specialize in] | [Key skills or industries].
Pack in searchable keywords a recruiter would type. Make it specific and
confident, not buzzwordy.

MY ROLE: [title]
MY SPECIALTIES/SKILLS: [list]
INDUSTRIES I WANT: [list]
```

Example output: *"Marketing Manager | Demand Gen & Lifecycle Email | SaaS &
E-commerce | HubSpot, SQL, Paid Social"*

---

## The About section (your story in their keywords)

```
Write a LinkedIn "About" section in first person, 3 short paragraphs:
1. A hook line stating who I am and the value I deliver.
2. My background and 2–3 standout achievements with numbers.
3. What I'm looking for / how to reach me, plus a keyword-rich skills line.

Tone: confident and human, like I'm talking to one person. Include the keywords
below naturally. Under 1,500 characters. Use line breaks for skimmability.

KEYWORDS: [skills + industry terms]
MY ACHIEVEMENTS: [paste a few]
WHAT I WANT NEXT: [one line]
```

## Experience bullets (reuse your resume, reformat for LinkedIn)

```
Convert my resume bullets into LinkedIn experience entries. Slightly more
narrative than a resume, still results-driven, still keyword-rich. Add a one-line
context sentence about the role before the bullets.

MY RESUME BULLETS: [paste]
```

---

## The 20-minute optimization checklist

- [ ] **Photo:** a clear, friendly headshot. *(A phone selfie in good light
      against a plain wall is fine — no studio, no camera gear.)* Profiles with
      photos get vastly more views.
- [ ] **Headline:** keyword-rich, from the prompt above.
- [ ] **About:** filled in, skimmable, keyword-rich.
- [ ] **Skills:** add 15–25. Pin your top 3 to match your target roles.
      Recruiters filter by these.
- [ ] **Open to Work:** turn it on (you can make it visible to recruiters only,
      so your current employer doesn't see it).
- [ ] **Custom URL:** edit it to `linkedin.com/in/firstlast`.
- [ ] **Location:** set to the area you're targeting (or "Open to relocate").
- [ ] **Featured:** pin 1–2 things — a portfolio link, a post, a project.
- [ ] **Connections:** you need 50+ to look active. Connect with people in your
      target field/companies.

## The recruiter-magnet move

```
Based on the type of role I want (below), give me 15 hashtags and 20 keywords I
should sprinkle across my LinkedIn profile and posts so I appear in recruiter
searches and feed algorithms for this field.

TARGET ROLE: [role]
INDUSTRY: [industry]
```

Drop those keywords into your About, Skills, and any posts. This is how you get
found without ever applying.
