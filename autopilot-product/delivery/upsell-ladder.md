# The Upsell Ladder — more revenue per visitor

The fastest path to $15K/month usually isn't more traffic — it's more money from
the traffic you already have. The same 500 visitors can produce 2–3× the revenue
if you give buyers a natural next step. Build these *after* the $27 product is
live and selling; don't delay launch for them.

---

## The three rungs

| Rung | Price | What it is | When it's offered |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Front-end** | $27 | The AI Job Search Toolkit (you already built this) | The main sale |
| **Order bump** | $17 | A focused add-on, one checkbox at checkout | At checkout |
| **Upsell** | $47–$97 | A bigger "done-with-you" or expansion product | Right after purchase |

### Why this multiplies revenue
If 100 people buy the $27 product, and 30% take a $17 bump and 10% take a $47
upsell, you go from **$2,700 → $3,680** from the *same traffic*. That's a 36%
revenue lift with zero extra visitors.

---

## Order-bump ideas ($17) — pick one, build it from AI

- **"The 100 ATS Keywords Cheat Sheet"** — a ready reference of the highest-value
  resume keywords by field. (AI generates this in an afternoon.)
- **"The Recruiter Outreach Pack"** — 20 fill-in-the-blank LinkedIn/email
  templates to message hiring managers directly.
- **"The 7-Day Job Search Sprint"** — a day-by-day checklist that tells them
  exactly what to do each day for a week.

## Upsell ideas ($47–$97)

- **"The Career Pivot Edition"** — an expanded version for people changing
  industries: transferable-skill mapping, narrative scripts, networking plans.
- **"The Interview Mastery Pack"** — 200 role-specific questions with model
  answers, plus advanced salary negotiation role-plays.
- **"Lifetime Prompt Vault"** — a continually expanding library of career prompts
  (resume, LinkedIn, networking, promotions, raises) — positioned as the thing
  they keep using for their whole career.

---

## The most important upgrade of all: a free email capture

Most visitors won't buy on day one. Capture them so you can sell later for free.

- Offer a **free lead magnet** ("The 1-Page Resume Checklist" or "5 ATS-Killer
  Resume Mistakes") in exchange for an email.
- Use a free email tool (MailerLite and Brevo both have generous free tiers).
- Send a short 4–5 email sequence that helps them, then offers the $27 product.

**An email list is the closest thing to a renewable, free traffic source you
will ever own.** Every subscriber is someone you can re-offer to — the upsells
above, a future product, or just the front-end again — at zero cost. Over time
this list can become the single biggest driver toward that $15K number.

---

## Sequencing (don't do it all at once)

1. **Week 1:** Launch the $27 product. Get the first sales. Prove the funnel.
2. **Week 2–3:** Add the email capture + free lead magnet. Start the list.
3. **Week 4+:** Add the $17 order bump (fastest revenue win).
4. **Month 2+:** Build the $47–$97 upsell once you know which audience is buying.

Ship the core first. A live $27 product beats a perfect ladder that never
launches.
