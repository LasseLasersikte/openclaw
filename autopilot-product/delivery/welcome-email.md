# The Automated Welcome Email

Paste this into your Gumroad product's post-purchase email / receipt content. It
sends itself on every sale. It does three jobs: deliver the product, reduce
refunds by getting them a quick win, and open the door to the next purchase.

---

**Subject:** Your AI Job Search Toolkit is here 🎉 (start with this one file)

---

Hey, and congrats — you just did the smartest thing a job seeker can do: stop
guessing and grab a system.

**Your download is attached / available right here:** [download link]

Here's how to get a win in the next 20 minutes:

1. Open **`00-START-HERE`** first. It's your 2-minute map.
2. Grab one job posting you actually want to apply to.
3. Run **Prompt 1 in `01-ats-resume-system`** to see exactly what that employer's
   screening software is scanning for. That single step is an eye-opener.

That's it. Don't try to do everything tonight — just get one resume dialed in and
you'll feel the difference immediately.

**A small ask:** if this helps you, reply and tell me when you land an interview.
I read every message and it genuinely makes my day.

You've got this,
[Your name]

P.S. — Want me to do even more of the heavy lifting? [If you set up an upsell or
a follow-up offer, mention it here in one friendly line. Otherwise delete this.]

---

## Why each part is here (don't skip these)

- **Immediate "do this one thing" instruction** → buyers who get a quick win in
  the first 24 hours almost never refund. This is your refund insurance.
- **The reply prompt** → real replies build trust, surface testimonials you can
  use on the sales page, and train Gumroad/email deliverability.
- **The P.S.** → the highest-read line in any email. Reserve it for your next
  offer once you have one (see `upsell-ladder.md`).
