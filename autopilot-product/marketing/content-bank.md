## B) Value posts (short-form: X / Threads / LinkedIn)

11. **Beat the ATS in one step:** paste the job posting into AI and ask it to
    "extract the 15 keywords an ATS will scan for." Now you know exactly what to
    mirror in your resume. Most people never do this.

12. **Kill "responsible for."** Every resume bullet should be:
    Action verb + what you did + how + measurable result.
    "Responsible for social media" → "Grew Instagram 3x to 40k in 6 months via a
    daily content system." Same job. Different candidate.

13. **The cover letter test:** if you could swap in any other company's name and
    it still works, it's a generic letter and it's getting ignored. Name the
    company in sentence one and say something true about it.

14. **LinkedIn is a search engine.** Recruiters type keywords. If your profile
    doesn't contain the words they search, you don't exist. Load your headline,
    About, and Skills with the exact terms from jobs you want.

15. **Interview prep in 5 minutes:** ask AI, "You're the hiring manager for [job].
    List the 12 questions you'd most likely ask." You'll be shocked how accurate
    it is. Rehearse those answers. Done.

16. **Never name your salary number first.** When asked your expectations: "What
    range has the team budgeted for this role?" Let them anchor.

17. **The STAR method** turns rambling into a hire: Situation, Task, Action,
    Result. Build 5 STAR stories and you can answer almost any behavioral
    question.

18. **Quantify everything.** Numbers are the difference between "I helped with
    marketing" and "I drove a 22% lift in qualified leads." Even estimates beat
    vague.

19. **One-column resumes only.** Fancy two-column templates look great to you and
    scramble in the screening software. Boring and readable beats pretty and
    rejected.

20. **Turn on "Open to Work" — recruiter-only mode.** You become visible to
    recruiters without your current boss knowing. Free inbound opportunities.

## How to use this bank efficiently

- **Batch once a week.** Sit down, turn 10 of these into Canva pins + scheduled
  posts in one session. Then the channel runs hands-off for the week.
- **Reword and repost.** The same tip works as a pin title, a tweet, a LinkedIn
  post, and a Reddit answer. You are not "repeating yourself" — different people
  see each one.
- **Let the product write more.** Every prompt and tip in `../product/` is raw
  material. Pull a line, deliver the value, link the toolkit. You will never run
  out of content because the content *is* the product.
- **Track which posts get clicks**, then make 5 more like the winners. That feed-
  back loop is how a trickle becomes the 500/day in the README's table.
