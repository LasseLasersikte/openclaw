## ✅ Before a human ever sees it (the robot test)

- [ ] **One column only.** No side-by-side layouts, tables, or text boxes for
      anything important — screening software scrambles them.
- [ ] **Standard section headers:** *Summary · Experience · Skills · Education.*
      The robot looks for these exact words.
- [ ] **Keywords mirrored from the job posting.** If the posting says
      "stakeholder management," your resume says it too (where it's true).
- [ ] **Standard font** (Calibri, Arial, Georgia), 10–12pt. No graphics-as-text.
- [ ] **Saved as `.docx` or PDF** — match what the application asks for; `.docx`
      is safest for ATS.
- [ ] **Filename is `First-Last-Resume`** — not `resume_final_v3`.

## ✅ When a human reads it (the 7-second test)

- [ ] **Every bullet starts with an action verb** (Led, Built, Cut, Drove,
      Launched) — never "Responsible for."
- [ ] **At least 5 quantified results.** Numbers, %, $, time saved. "Grew X 3× in
      6 months" beats "helped with X."
- [ ] **A 3-sentence summary at the top**, tailored to the specific job.
- [ ] **One page** if under ~10 years' experience; two pages max otherwise.
- [ ] **Zero typos.** Paste it into any free AI: *"Proofread for spelling and
      grammar. List only the errors."*
- [ ] **No fluff words:** "team player," "hard worker," "detail-oriented,"
      "fast-paced." Cut them all — they say nothing.
