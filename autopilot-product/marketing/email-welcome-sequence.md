## Why this sequence works (don't change the structure)

- **Value before the ask.** Emails 1–2 give real wins with zero pitch. By the
  time you offer in email 3, you've earned it.
- **One idea per email.** Each is short and skimmable. Long emails don't get read.
- **The offer escalates gently:** soft mention (3) → stakes (4) → direct ask (5).
- **Every email helps even if they never buy** — that's what keeps people
  subscribed and opening, so your *next* product lands too.
- **Replies are gold.** They boost deliverability and hand you testimonials for
  the sales page. Always invite them.
