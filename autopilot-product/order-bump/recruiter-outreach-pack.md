## How to use this pack

1. Find the right person (a recruiter, hiring manager, or someone on the team)
   via LinkedIn search.
2. Pick the template that matches your situation below.
3. Fill the `[brackets]` — or paste the template + your details into free AI with:
   *"Personalize this message for [name], [title] at [company]. Keep it under 80
   words, warm and specific, not desperate."*
4. Keep it short. The goal of message #1 is a reply, not a job.

**The golden rule:** lead with something specific and true about *them* or the
company. Generic "I'm interested in opportunities" messages get ignored.

## Part 1 — Cold outreach to a recruiter (5 templates)

**1. The direct intro**
> Hi [Name] — I saw [Company] is hiring for [role] and it lines up closely with
> my background in [skill/area]. I recently [one specific achievement with a
> number]. Would you be open to a quick chat about whether I'd be a fit?

**2. The "I did my homework"**
> Hi [Name] — I've been following [Company]'s work on [specific product/project]
> and it's exactly the kind of [problem area] I love solving. I bring [X years]
> in [field] and [one result]. Are you the right person to talk to about [role]?

**3. The value-first**
> Hi [Name] — noticed you're hiring for [role]. In my last role I [solved a
> problem that role likely faces, with a result]. Happy to share how I'd approach
> it at [Company] if that's useful. Open to connecting?

**4. The referral-ask (when you're not sure they hire for your area)**
> Hi [Name] — I'm exploring [type of] roles at [Company] and saw you recruit
> there. Even if it's not your area, could you point me toward the right person?
> Quick background: [one line + one achievement]. Really appreciate it.

**5. The follow-up (3–4 days, no reply)**
> Hi [Name] — just floating this back up in case it slipped by. Still very
> interested in [role] at [Company]. Happy to send my resume or jump on a quick
> call whenever works. Thanks either way!

## Part 2 — Direct to the hiring manager / team (5 templates)

**6. The peer-to-peer**
> Hi [Name] — I see you lead [team] at [Company]. I'm a [your role] who's spent
> the last [X years] on [relevant work], including [achievement]. If you're
> growing the team, I'd love to throw my hat in. Worth a quick conversation?

**7. The problem-solver**
> Hi [Name] — congrats on [recent company news/launch]. Scaling [that] usually
> means [a challenge]. That's squarely what I do — at [past company] I [result].
> Would it be worth 15 minutes to see if I could help at [Company]?

**8. The warm-admirer**
> Hi [Name] — I've admired [Company]'s approach to [specific thing] for a while.
> I'm a [role] with experience in [area] and I'd jump at the chance to contribute.
> Are you open to candidates right now?

**9. The "applied but reaching out too"**
> Hi [Name] — I just applied for [role] on your team and wanted to reach out
> directly since I'm genuinely excited about it. Short version: [one line + one
> result]. I'd love the chance to talk it through whenever suits you.

**10. The post-engagement (after liking/commenting on their content)**
> Hi [Name] — really enjoyed your post on [topic]; your point about [detail]
> resonated. I work in [area] and [Company] is exactly where I'd want to do my
> best work. If you're hiring [role], I'd love to connect.

## Part 3 — Networking & informational (5 templates)

**11. The coffee-chat ask**
> Hi [Name] — I'm exploring a move into [field/role] and you've clearly built a
> great career in it. Would you be open to a 15-minute chat? I'd love to hear how
> you got into it. No ask beyond your perspective.

**12. The alumni/shared-connection**
> Hi [Name] — fellow [school/company/community]! I'm moving toward [role] and saw
> your path at [Company]. Could I ask you a couple of quick questions about it?
> Totally understand if you're slammed.

**13. The reconnect (old contact)**
> Hi [Name] — it's been a while! I've been doing [current focus] and I'm starting
> to look at [type of] roles. If anything at [their company] or your network comes
> to mind, I'd be grateful for the nudge. Would love to catch up regardless.

**14. The thank-you-after-a-chat**
> Hi [Name] — thank you for the time today, genuinely helpful. Your advice on
> [specific point] is something I'll act on this week. If a relevant role ever
> crosses your desk, I'd love to be considered. Staying in touch!

**15. The "keep me in mind"**
> Hi [Name] — really enjoyed connecting. I'm actively looking for [role] focused
> on [area]. If you hear of anything — at [Company] or elsewhere — I'd appreciate
> the heads up. Happy to return the favor anytime.

## Part 4 — Follow-ups & closers (5 templates)

**16. The post-interview thank-you**
> Hi [Name] — thank you for the conversation about [role]. I left even more
> excited, especially about [specific thing discussed]. I'm confident I'd make an
> impact on [goal]. Happy to provide anything else you need — looking forward to
> next steps.

**17. The status-check (after an interview, no word)**
> Hi [Name] — hope you're well. Just checking in on the [role] process and
> reaffirming my strong interest. I'm happy to answer any remaining questions.
> Any update on timing would be much appreciated.

**18. The "I have another offer" nudge**
> Hi [Name] — wanted to give you a heads-up: I've received another offer with a
> decision deadline of [date]. [Company] remains my top choice, so if there's any
> way to align timelines, I'd love to make it work here.

**19. The reconnect after a rejection**
> Hi [Name] — thank you for letting me know. I really enjoyed learning about
> [Company] and I'd welcome the chance to be considered for future roles in
> [area]. Please keep me in mind — I'll be cheering the team on regardless.

**20. The long-game touchpoint (months later)**
> Hi [Name] — circling back since we spoke a while ago. I've since [new
> achievement/skill] and [Company] is still very much on my radar. If you're
> growing [team] again, I'd love to reconnect.

## The one AI prompt that powers the whole pack

```
Personalize this outreach message. Keep it under 80 words, warm, specific, and
confident — never desperate. Open with something true about the person or company
from the details I provide. End with a low-friction ask (a reply or a quick chat).

TEMPLATE: [paste one above]
PERSON: [name, title, company]
SOMETHING SPECIFIC ABOUT THEM/COMPANY: [a post, a launch, a value, a connection]
MY 1 BEST RELEVANT ACHIEVEMENT: [paste]
```

Send 5 of these a day and you'll generate more conversations in a week than most
people get from a month of applying into the void.
