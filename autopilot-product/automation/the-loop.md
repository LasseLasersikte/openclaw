# The Loop — what runs itself vs. what you touch

This is the honest map of "autopilot." A digital product business has a part that
is genuinely hands-off and a part that needs a human about an hour a day. Knowing
which is which keeps you from quitting.

---

## Fully automated (runs 24/7, zero touch)

Once live, these need no human:

- **The sales page** serves itself to anyone, anytime.
- **Checkout & payment** (Gumroad) — takes the money, handles tax/VAT, sends the
  receipt.
- **Delivery** — the buyer gets the download + welcome email instantly.
- **Order bump / upsell** — extra revenue captured automatically at checkout.
- **Pinterest pins & scheduled posts you already published** keep pulling traffic
  while you sleep.
- **Email sequences** (if you set up the free list) nurture and sell on a timer.

A sale can begin and complete — payment, delivery, follow-up — with you doing
absolutely nothing. That part of the promise is real.

---

## Needs you (~1 hour/day, the engine of the whole thing)

- **Feeding traffic.** Posting/pinning daily and answering community questions.
  This is the input the automated machine converts into money. (See
  `../marketing/traffic-playbook.md`.) Over time, search/Pinterest/email shift
  more of this to passive — but it never goes fully to zero.
- **Reading the numbers weekly** and doubling down on what converts.
- **Replying to buyers** and the occasional refund/support email.

---

## The weekly operating rhythm

**Monday (batch day, ~90 min)**
- Make 10–15 Pinterest pins + write the week's short-form posts in one sitting.
- Schedule them across the week (free schedulers exist for most platforms).

**Tue–Fri (~30–45 min/day)**
- Answer 2–3 Reddit/Quora questions genuinely.
- Post the day's scheduled content (or let the scheduler do it).
- Reply to any buyer emails.

**Friday (~30 min — the review)**
- Check: visitors, sales, conversion rate, best channel (use per-channel links).
- Write down the one number you'll try to improve next week.

**Monthly (~1–2 hrs)**
- Publish one durable asset (blog post or faceless video).
- Add or refine one rung of the upsell ladder.
- Cut the weakest channel; pour its time into the best one.

---

## The 60-day rule

Compounding channels (Pinterest, SEO, email) take **weeks** to gain momentum.
Plan to run the loop for 60 days before judging it. The people who hit the income
table are simply the ones who kept feeding the machine while the slow channels
matured. The machine is built. Whether it scales is now a function of consistency
— and that part is genuinely in your hands.

---

## Reality check, one more time

- The system is **complete and real**: product, page, checkout, delivery, traffic
  plan.
- The **$15K/month number is a target, not a guarantee** — it's traffic ×
  conversion × price, and you control the work that drives traffic and the offer
  that drives conversion.
- The honest expectation: first sales in **days to weeks** with consistent
  posting; meaningful, compounding income over **months** if you keep going and
  keep improving the page and the offer based on the numbers.

Build it once. Feed it daily. Improve it weekly. That's the loop.
