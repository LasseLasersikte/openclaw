## Read this first (the honest part)

You were sold a number: **$15K/month on autopilot**. Here is the truth, because
a system built on a lie collapses the first month.

- **"Autopilot" is real — but only the back half.** Once it's live, the product,
  the page, the checkout, and the delivery run themselves 24/7 with zero human
  touch. That part is genuinely hands-off.
- **The front half — traffic — is not free money, it's free *work*.** No camera
  required, but attention has to come from *somewhere*. This system uses free
  channels (search, Pinterest, Reddit, quora, email). That's labor up front
  that decays into passive once it ranks/compounds.
- **Nobody can promise $15K/month.** What's honest is the *math* (below). Hitting
  it is a function of traffic × conversion × price, and you control two of three.

If you want the fantasy, close this folder. If you want the actual machine,
keep reading.

## The economics, in plain numbers

Product price: **$27**. After Gumroad/Stripe fees (~$2), you net **~$25/sale**.

| Monthly goal | Sales needed | Sales/day | Visitors/day @ 2% conv. | Visitors/day @ 4% conv. |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| $1,000  | 40    | ~1.3  | ~65    | ~33   |
| $5,000  | 200   | ~6.7  | ~335   | ~167  |
| $15,000 | 600   | 20    | ~1,000 | ~500  |

**$15K/month = 20 sales/day = roughly 500–1,000 visitors/day.** That is the real
target. It is reachable, but it is a traffic problem, not a magic problem. Most
people quit at the traffic step — which is exactly why the [marketing
playbook](marketing/traffic-playbook.md) is the most important file here, not
the product.

Two honest levers if 500 visitors/day feels far:
1. **Raise average order value.** A $27 front-end + a $47 order-bump + a $97
   upsell can triple revenue per visitor. (Bump and upsell ideas are in
   [delivery/upsell-ladder.md](delivery/upsell-ladder.md).)
2. **Stack channels.** One channel rarely does 1,000 visits/day. Five channels
   doing 100–200 each, compounding monthly, gets there.

## Do-this-now checklist (launch in one afternoon)

1. **Skim the product** in `product/` so you can stand behind it. Optionally
   export the `.md` files to PDF (any free Markdown→PDF tool, or print-to-PDF).
2. **Create a free Gumroad account** and follow
   [delivery/gumroad-setup.md](delivery/gumroad-setup.md). Upload the product,
   set price $27, paste the welcome email. ~20 minutes.
3. **Deploy the sales page.** Open `sales-page/index.html`, replace the one
   `BUY_URL` placeholder with your Gumroad link, and host it free (Vercel,
   Netlify, Cloudflare Pages, or GitHub Pages). ~10 minutes. *(Or skip it —
   Gumroad gives you a hosted product page out of the box.)*
4. **Turn on traffic.** Open [marketing/traffic-playbook.md](marketing/traffic-playbook.md)
   and post the first 5 pieces from [marketing/content-bank.md](marketing/content-bank.md).
   This is the part that decides whether you hit the table above.
5. **Run the weekly loop** in [automation/the-loop.md](automation/the-loop.md).

That's the whole machine. The product sells for $27, delivers itself, and the
only recurring input is traffic — which compounds if you keep feeding it.
